Account deletion

You can delete your Sranks AI account from within the app.

Delete from the app

  1. Open Sranks AI and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Carefully read the confirmation and complete the deletion steps.

Can't access your account?

Send an email to support.sranksai@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. Use the subject “Sranks AI account deletion”. Include your account email address, but never include your password or verification codes.

What is deleted?

Account deletion is intended to delete your sign-in account and associated Sranks AI data, including chats, messages, projects, saved files, profile information and settings stored in our database. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or security logs, or be retained where required by law. Information already processed or held by Google or Render is governed by those providers’ own retention and deletion policies.

Need help?

Email support.sranksai@gmail.com.
