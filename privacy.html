2. Information We Collect and Process

Depending on the features you use, Sranks AI may collect or process the following categories of information:

We use information processed through Sranks AI for the following purposes:

Sranks AI uses third-party technology providers to deliver certain AI-powered features.

Depending on the feature and application version, information may be processed through the following services:
